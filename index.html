About Blog Write

hi! i'm trong khang — a 12th grader in ho chi minh city, currently splitting my time between school, a laptop, and a very patient group of students i mentor.

i got into programming the usual way: a small problem i wanted to solve, then another one, then a pile of half-finished projects. somewhere along the way it turned into wanting to understand how intelligence works — in machines and in people — which is still the question i keep circling back to.

these days i build things that scratch my own itches: ai-dermdiag, a skin-lesion classifier i keep tuning (pytorch, grad-cam, and more late nights staring at confusion matrices than i'd like to admit); a small real-time fact-checking agent that started as a hackathon joke and somehow won one; and a tick-level c++ backtesting engine i've rewritten more times than i can count.

i also spend a lot of time teaching — mentoring younger students in programming and research methodology at bioturing, and running stem workshops at ichc. it's the fastest way i know to find out whether i actually understand something.

next year i'm heading into maths & computer science, with an eye on modelling and scientific computing — the kind of work where you write code to understand a physical system, not just to ship a feature. i'm still working out the exact shape of that, one project at a time.

outside of code: vovinam (a few district and city medals, mostly a good excuse to get hit), percussion, long walks with a problem stuck in my head, and an esp32 board that has been "almost finished" for months.

i'm always happy to talk to people who build — a question, an idea, a project you're stuck on, or just want to say hi? send me a message!
